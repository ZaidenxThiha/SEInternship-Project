# WATA Software

WATA Software is a prominent IT outsourcing and software development company based in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam, established in 2015. Over the past decade, the firm has grown into a highly reliable technology partner, employing hundreds of skilled engineers who serve a diverse global clientele across North America, Australia, Japan, South Korea, Singapore, and the domestic Vietnamese market.

Led by Founder and CEO Dam Van Thien, WATA Software specializes in delivering end-to-end software solutions, including custom web and mobile application development, enterprise SaaS platforms, cloud migration (via AWS and Azure), DevOps automation, and specialized Big Data analytics.

The company is particularly recognized for its ability to integrate cutting-edge technologies like Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning, and Internet of Things (IoT) into practical business applications for industries such as fintech, e-commerce, and logistics.

By leveraging Vietnam's competitive tech talent pool, WATA Software provides scalable, high-quality dedicated development teams and technology consulting that help international enterprises accelerate their digital transformation goals.
